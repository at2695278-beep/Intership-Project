About IPL

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is one of the world's most popular T20 cricket leagues. Founded in 2008, it brings together top cricket players from India and around the world. With exciting matches, passionate fans, and intense team rivalries, IPL has become a major part of Indian cricket.
